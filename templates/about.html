About FTI

🌱
Farm Tech Intelligence
Version 1.0

FTI helps farmers plan smarter by combining farm record-keeping — fields, crops, soil tests, harvests — with real-time weather and regional market oversupply insights, so you know what your neighbors are planting before you decide what to grow next.

Built With

Flask, SQLite, OpenWeatherMap, and Leaflet/OpenStreetMap.
