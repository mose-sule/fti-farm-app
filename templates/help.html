Getting Started

Add your first farm from the "Add Farm" button on your dashboard, then add fields, crops, and start logging activities and harvests as you go.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't I see weather on my dashboard?

Weather requires a farm location. Add or edit a farm and set its location on the map.

What does the Market Insights supply badge mean?

It shows how many farms in your market area are currently growing the same crop, helping you avoid oversupplying a crop that many neighbors are already planting.

Can I manage more than one farm?

Yes — add multiple farms from the Farms tab, and switch between them from the menu.
